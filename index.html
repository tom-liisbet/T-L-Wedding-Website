Everything you
need to know

The celebration takes place Saturday, September 5, 2026 at Jõekääru, an Estonian summer camp on the Pefferlaw River in Udora, Ontario — about an hour north of Toronto.

The ceremony begins at 5:00 PM, with dinner at 7:00 PM and dancing late into the night. Staying at the hotel block in Newmarket? A shuttle bus departs the hotel at 4:00 PM and return shuttles run from 10:00 PM until 1:00 AM — or skip the ride home entirely and spend the night on the property in a cabin, tent, or RV.

The Place

A camp, a river,
and a love story

Jõekääru

Jõekääru is the Estonian summer camp where Liisbet spent her childhood summers as a camper and counsellor — and, in a roundabout way, how she met Tom. We're beyond lucky to be married there, surrounded by forest, the Pefferlaw River, and years of fond memories.

The ceremony itself is a short walk from the reception, across two of the camp's footbridges — just like the one in this photo.
