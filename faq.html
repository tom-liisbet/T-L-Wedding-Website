Questions & Answers

FAQs

Can't find your answer here? Reach us anytime at tomandliisbet@gmail.com.

Will transportation be provided?

We will be providing a shuttle bus from the Comfort Inn to the venue before the start of the ceremony, as well as at various points following the reception. Ride share apps and taxis are limited. See the schedule for shuttle times.

What are we eating? What if I have a dietary restriction or food allergy?

Arguably the most important question! We are beyond thrilled to offer a selection of appetizers during cocktail hours, as well as a family-style dinner of pork schnitzel, roast chicken thighs, potatoes, and salads, and plenty of spicy Estonian mustard.

If you have a dietary restriction or allergy, there is a space in the RSVP to note it, but feel free to email us at tomandliisbet@gmail.com to discuss further.

Open bar?

Open bar!

What is the dress code?

Please dress for the weather! The wedding will be outdoors and take place entirely on grass or crushed gravel. Stilettos or other wobbly high heels may not be the best idea.

With regards to style, please dress garden party formal, with earth tones encouraged. We kindly ask no white/cream/ivory dresses.

Can I bring a plus one? What about my children?

Unfortunately only those named on the invite can be accommodated, due to health and safety codes established by the township. If you feel we left out someone important, please email us at tomandliisbet@gmail.com, but understand that due to limitations our hands may be tied.

Is there parking available?

There is ample parking available at the venue, marked "P" on the map (at the bottom left). Cars may be left there throughout the weekend. We ask guests to please not drive anywhere on the property other than the parking lot, for safety purposes.

When can I arrive to set up my tent / claim the top bunk / soak in the atmosphere?

Guests staying on the property can start arriving at 1:30 PM on the day of the wedding. See the map for tenting, cabin, and RV locations — cabin spots are first-come first-served, so the top bunk goes to the swift! Communal showers are located in the sauna.
