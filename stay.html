Accommodation

Where to stay

We're so excited to party all night long with you, but we understand everyone's dancing shoes wear out sooner or later. Please note on your RSVP if you are planning on spending the night on the property, and see below for specific information! Locations mentioned refer to the property map.

Hotel

Comfort Inn · Newmarket

We've secured a group rate at the Comfort Inn in Newmarket for September 4th and 5th. When booking over the phone, please mention the "Tiisler Wedding" to access the group rate.

Unfortunately, early check-in (before 3pm) is not available, which is why we've secured the same rate for the night before the wedding as well. However you don't need to book both nights to access the group rate.

Please book your hotel stay ASAP, as due to the holiday weekend they can only hold the rooms until July 29th. After this day, the group rate is still available over the phone, but based on room availability.

There are other hotels in the Newmarket area as well, specifically the Fairfield by Marriott which is just down the street from the Comfort Inn (~200m).

A shuttle bus runs between the hotel block and the venue — it departs the hotel at 4:00 PM, and return shuttles run from 10:00 PM until 1:00 AM. See the schedule.

Tenting

On the property

Please park at the main parking lot on the West side of the river and make your way over to the cabin area on foot. The designated tenting area is between the flagpoles and volleyball courts (see orange area with dotted outline and tent icon on the map). Please use the cabins' bathrooms.

Cabins

On the property

Please park at the main parking lot on the West side of the river and make your way over to the cabin area on foot. All cabins have a mix of bunk beds and single or double rooms, and spots are available on a first-come first-served basis.

Please bring bedding (twin size) or a sleeping bag, and maybe some earplugs! +2 points for spotting Liisbet's name written in the back room of the girls' cabin!
